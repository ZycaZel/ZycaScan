01 — The pipeline

Four steps between the whole market and a short list.

Most charts on most days aren't doing anything worth a second look. The pipeline exists to throw those away mechanically, so the list you read is short and every name on it says why it's there.

01 / Universe

Every listed common stock

Built from Nasdaq Trader's symbol directories: common shares, ordinary shares and ADRs. No ETFs, warrants, units, rights, notes or preferreds.

02 / Liquidity

Tradeable, or it's out

Last close at least $5 and at least 1,000,000 shares traded in the last session, with 30+ sessions of history to measure against.

03 / Setups

Five rules, applied blind

20-day breakout, bull flag, 20-day average reclaim, oversold, oversold bounce — computed on daily closes, the same way for every chart.

04 / Publish

Listed, charted, logged

Every match goes on the list with its rule, its 3-month chart and its RSI. What's new, what changed and what dropped off is logged run to run.

Before you read anything into it

Questions worth asking.

Is this a list of stocks to buy?
No. It's a list of charts that matched a mechanical rule on the last close. A setup is a description of what a chart did, not a prediction of what it will do, and plenty of breakouts fail the next day. ZycaScan doesn't know your money, your risk, or your plan.
Why are there hundreds of matches?
Because the rules are deliberately simple and run on daily closes only. On a strong day hundreds of charts close at a 20-day high; on a weak one, hundreds go oversold. Use the filters, look at confluence, and start from names you already know.
Where does the data come from, and how fresh is it?
The symbol list comes from Nasdaq Trader's public directories; prices come from Yahoo Finance's free chart data, which is unofficial and can lag or be wrong. The scan runs on completed daily closes, so it describes the last session, not the market right now. The timestamp on every page says which close it is.
Does this site place trades?
No. There is no account, no broker connection and no order anywhere on this site. If you run the scanner yourself as Claude skills, orders go to a paper account by default and only after a risk check passes and you type an explicit yes for that one order.
How do the breakout alerts work, and where do charts come from?
Each night the scan sets a 20-day-high level for up to 150 candidates. Every 5 minutes during market hours, a 5-minute bar that closes above its level (or above the opening range) on at least 1.5× normal volume is posted to Discord once and listed in the Live section. Detection runs on free Yahoo data. The interactive charts in the explorer are TradingView's own embedded widget, on TradingView's data, so prices can differ slightly. See the methodology for the exact rules.
What does "Replay" show?
What each past scan listed, and the change from the close it was flagged at to the latest close. Close to close, no costs, no slippage, and only as many scans as have been run. It's a record, not a backtest and not a track record.
