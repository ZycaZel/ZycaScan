About

A sister project to ZycaAlgo, reading charts instead of filings.

ZycaAlgo reads every SEC Form 4 to find insiders buying their own stock. ZycaScan asks a different question of the same market: which charts did something worth a second look on the last close?

Why it exists

Flipping through five thousand charts by hand every evening isn't realistic, and most screeners hide their rules behind a score. ZycaScan applies five plain rules to every liquid US chart, shows you exactly which rule each one met, and keeps a log of what changed from one scan to the next.

It is a research project. It's free, there's no account, and nothing on the site trades or connects to a broker.

What it is not

  • Not a list of stocks to buy, and not a prediction. A setup describes what a chart did.
  • Not real time. Every page names the daily close it was built from.
  • Not a track record. Replay is a close-to-close record of past lists, with no trading rules, costs or slippage.
A scan is a list of charts that match a rule, not a list of stocks to buy.
